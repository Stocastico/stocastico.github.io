Now

A snapshot of what I'm focused on at the moment. It's the kind of thing I'd tell you if we met for a coffee and you asked, "so, what are you up to these days?"

I don't want to jinx it, but things seem to be slowly getting better: our boy's sleep is a bit more regular and he doesn't wake up so often at night. That means we sleep a bit more and have more energy during the day. I can feel it at work (I'm far more productive now than just a couple of months ago) and at home too: once the little one is in bed we can enjoy a proper dinner, we're back to watching series and films, and I've even picked The Brothers Karamazov back up (40% in, and a great book so far).

I also read a short book by George Gamow, Thirty Years That Shook Physics: The Story of Quantum Theory. Physics has always fascinated me, and if I ever find the time I'd like to study it a bit more, ideally to get a deeper understanding of what I learned at university years ago.

The end of the year is getting close, which means we're planning our Christmas trips. We'll be in Italy for a week around New Year, and we're also planning the (at least in my family) traditional trip to the Canary Islands in January. We're also organising a longer trip for next spring, before our son turns two and needs a full-price plane ticket 🙂

And one more thing: I've redesigned this website. It's a bit more pleasant to look at, and more in line with my current job at a big media company.
