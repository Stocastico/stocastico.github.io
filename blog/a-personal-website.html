A personal website

Introduction

After witnessing the increasing abilities of Claude Code I decided to give it a go and use it to develop a bunch of a project ideas I had in mind since a long time but I never really had the time to implement. This website is one of them.

Main Section

I am not a web developer but I have always liked the idea of creating a personal space where I could share my thoughts, organize my ideas or simply add projects to my portfolio. In the past I learned the basics and built a now defunct personal blog, as well as another blog about food and lifestyle in Frankfurt am Main (back when I was living there) which got actually pretty successful. As the internet never forgets, it is still available on the Wayback Machine.

This time I wanted to be more ambitious, though. After doing the beautiful Three.js course by Bruno Simon, and learning a bit of React at work, I thought that with the help of Claude Code I could create something with a strong visual identity and a few nice 3D effects. What you are seeing right now is the results of these efforts.

The website is intended as my online business card, where people can get more information about my career without the need to check on LinkedIn. Right now it is only hosted on Github Pages, but maybe in the future I will buy a domain name or host it somewhere else, I am still not sure about that.

But why adding a blog, in 2026?

Personal blogs died more or less ten years ago, when social media became the de-facto standard for creating communities on the web. Many of the blogs I was following just stopped updating, while some others still post content from time to time, every time for smaller and smaller audiences though.

Nonetheless, I decided to try starting a new post mainly for myself: even if no-one stops by reading my mumblings, the act of writing will help me clear my ideas and remember something interesting I did, read or learned.

In the best case, though, I hope that the content I write can be interesting for others as well: in this age of enshittification and AI-slop I believe that offering content written by a human still has value. Maybe the topics I will talk about (which I assume will revolve around Generative AI, computer vision and research) are a niche of little interest to the majority of people... but even so I guees it is worth giving it a shot.

How often will I update the blog?

I really have no idea. Maybe I will get tired very soon. Maybe I will finally manage to sleep at night an I won't have time for adding new content. Or maybe I will enjoy more and more writing here, who knows? Right now, I aim for posting something once every couple months, let's see if I am able to maintain the pace.
