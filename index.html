I'm a Senior AI Engineer at GRUP MEDIAPRO, passionate about building intelligent systems that bridge cutting-edge research and real-world impact.

My work spans computer vision, deep learning, augmented reality, and multi-modal media analysis. I hold a PhD from the University of the Basque Country (UPV/EHU), where I researched collaborative, multi-user augmented reality experiences for education — a field that merges my love for technology with its transformative power in learning.

Previously I contributed to research at Vicomtech Foundation (Basque Research and Technology Alliance) and the Fraunhofer Institute for Telecommunications, working on projects ranging from automated psycholinguistics video annotation to audience engagement measurement and multi-modal speaker diarisation.

I'm driven by curiosity, open collaboration, and a belief that AI should serve people — making everyday experiences smarter, more accessible, and more human.
