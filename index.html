Who I Am

I'm a Senior AI Engineer at GRUP MEDIAPRO, passionate about building applications that bring cutting-edge research into production systems.

My work spans generative AI, deep learning, 3D reconstruction, and large language models. I hold a PhD from the University of the Basque Country, where I researched collaborative, multi-user augmented reality experiences for education.

I have worked in Italy, Germany, and Spain at both research institutions and software companies — from start-ups to large consulting firms. Along the way I picked up four foreign languages and a good number of programming ones too.

I'm driven by curiosity, collaboration, and passion. I believe that through our work and actions, especially now that AI is becoming increasingly pervasive, we should try to make the world a better place, one small step at a time.

Research

What I Work On

Want the full journey behind these projects? Dive into my full CV

Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning

Designing and deploying ML systems — from classical algorithms to modern foundation models — with a focus on production-ready pipelines and real-world performance.

Computer Vision & Deep Learning

Object detection, semantic segmentation, video understanding, and multi-modal perception. Building systems that help machines see and interpret the visual world.

Augmented Reality for Education

PhD research on collaborative, multi-user XR experiences that make learning more immersive and effective — from architecture design to large-scale pilots.

Multi-modal Media Analysis

Combining audio, video, and text signals for applications such as speaker diarisation, audience engagement measurement, and video lecture segmentation.

Video Understanding

End-to-end pipelines for lecture and conference video classification, topic visualisation, and automatic annotation of large audiovisual corpora.

Interactive & Collaborative Systems

Designing interoperable architectures for multi-user real-time experiences, interactive broadcast/broadband services, and connected media ecosystems.
